Room Guard

An AI agent that helps small hotels decide how much advance to take on direct bookings, collects it through Razorpay payment links, and refills rooms when guests cancel.

  1. Start on the setup screen, then open Rahul: his risk, the reasons, and the recommended advance.
  2. Tap Ask arrival time first, play his reply, and watch the risk and advance drop.
  3. Open Meena: high risk, so half the stay upfront. Change her rate from ₹4,000 to ₹5,000 and watch the advance update.
  4. Send Rahul's link, then play "The advance is too much": it comes back to you to offer a smaller advance. Then handle Deepak's unpaid advance.
  5. Simulate Priya's cancellation and refill the room from the waitlist.
  6. Check Farah in or mark a no-show, then see Impact, learning and the activity log.

Prototype with sample data. Risk points come from a scorecard learned on 12,606 direct bookings in an open hotel dataset (Antonio, de Almeida and Nunes, 2019). Guest drop-off rates, resale chances and the "no reply" signal are assumptions to be learned from real outcomes. Payments and WhatsApp are simulated.
